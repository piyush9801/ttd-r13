More than booking.

From the first idea to the right voice in the room.

Some clients need a booking partner. Others need help shaping a show, finding its host or building a strategy for what comes next.

We do both, and everything in between.

Seven services

  1. Talent Booking

    From research and outreach to negotiation and execution, we handle the entire booking process as an extension of your team.

  2. Strategy + Consulting

    Expert guidance on guest selection, timing and programming for teams managing their own bookings.

  3. Show Development

    Launching something new or rethinking an existing show? We help define its positioning, guest strategy and path forward.

  4. Talent + Host Strategy

    We identify the voices that fit your concept, audience and ambitions, then help bring them on board.

  5. Visibility + PR

    We help hosts reach new audiences through strategic appearances, media opportunities and thoughtful positioning.

  6. Special Projects

    Festivals, live events, tours, tentpole episodes and other programming that calls for a different approach.

  7. Global Expansion

    We help international creators and media companies navigate the U.S. market through local insight, strategy and industry relationships.
